# Module 04: Research & Plan Your Product

## What you'll learn

Good AI-assisted development starts before you write a single line of code. This module walks you through using Claude to do real market research, write a Product Requirements Document, break it into developer tickets, choose an architecture, design your database, and scaffold your project. You'll end this module with a complete plan Claude can execute.

## Lessons

| # | Lesson | Type | Time |
|---|--------|------|------|
| 1 | Market Research with Claude | exercise | 15 min |
| 2 | Writing a PRD | exercise | 18 min |
| 3 | Creating Your Tickets | exercise | 15 min |
| 4 | Architecture & Tech Stack | text | 20 min |
| 5 | Database Design | exercise | 15 min |
| 6 | Project Scaffolding | exercise | 10 min |

## By the end of this module

- You have a complete PRD and ticket list for the app you'll build in Module 6
- Your project is scaffolded with the right architecture and database schema ready to build against
